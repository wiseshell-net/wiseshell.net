Donate

Thanks for considering a donation!

If you enjoyed playing this game and would like to say thank you, invite me to a coffee, or help the project, you can donate!

Advantages

  • Pre-releases availability
  • In-Game Donor Mention (5€ or more)

Fees

Please be aware of the fees for low volume sales. This should be taken into consideration before you are donating small amounts of money.

Why donate?

Donations help us improve the game and keep it alive. Examples of what the money could be used for are:

  • Server for hosting websites
  • Game content
  • Development
  • Coffee
